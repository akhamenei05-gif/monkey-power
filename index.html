About Monkey Power

Satire in portrait form.

Monkey Power examines status, ambition, influence and spectacle through a consistent visual language. The project is independent, digital-first and built as an evolving archive.

This is a satirical art project. All works are fictionalized artistic interpretations and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any person, company or institution depicted or referenced.
